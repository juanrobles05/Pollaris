# Pollaris – Product Definition

## 1. Product Vision

Pollaris is a digital election management platform designed to help organizations conduct voting processes in a simple, transparent, and reliable way.

The goal of Pollaris is to provide a modern alternative to traditional voting methods such as paper ballots, manual counting, or generic form tools, enabling structured election processes with automated vote counting, voter control, and audit capabilities.

Pollaris focuses primarily on small and medium-scale elections within organizations, educational institutions, and communities.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Many voting processes in organizations and small communities are still conducted using traditional methods such as:

* Paper ballots
* Hand counting
* Raising hands during meetings
* Online forms like Google Forms

These approaches can introduce several problems:

* Human counting errors
* Lack of transparency
* Limited auditability
* Slow result generation
* Difficulty verifying voter participation

Organizations often need a structured and reliable way to manage elections without relying on complex or expensive systems.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

Pollaris provides a centralized digital platform that enables organizations to create and manage elections with structured workflows.

The system allows administrators to define elections, register candidates, manage voters, and conduct the voting process in a controlled and auditable environment.

Key capabilities include:

* Digital election creation
* Candidate management
* Voter registration and validation
* Secure vote submission
* Automated vote counting
* Election result visualization

The platform is designed with a modern architecture that allows future improvements such as high availability, distributed services, and audit mechanisms.

---

## 4. Target Users

Pollaris is designed for organizations that require structured voting processes.

Potential users include:

* Educational institutions (student councils, class representatives)
* Organizations and companies (internal decision making)
* Associations or clubs
* Community groups
* Online communities

The system is not initially intended for national or governmental elections.

---

## 5. Core Principles

Pollaris is built around the following design principles:

### Simplicity

The platform should be easy to use for both administrators and voters.

### Reliability

Votes must be recorded correctly and counted accurately.

### Transparency

Election processes and results should be clear and verifiable.

### Auditability

The system should allow verification of voting activity and results.

### Availability

The system should remain accessible during active election periods.

---

## 6. MVP Scope (Version 1)

The first version of Pollaris will focus on the core functionality required to run a simple election.

### Election Management

* Create elections
* Configure start and end dates

### Candidate Management

* Register candidates
* Associate candidates with elections

### Voter Management

* Register voters
* Validate voter participation

### Voting Process

* Allow a voter to submit a vote
* Prevent multiple votes from the same voter

### Results

* Automatic vote counting
* Display election results

---

## 7. Out of Scope (Initial Version)

The following features are not part of the initial release but may be considered in future versions:

* Government or national election support
* Biometric authentication
* External identity verification
* Public blockchain voting infrastructure

---

## 8. Future Evolution

Future versions of Pollaris may explore more advanced capabilities such as:

* Improved authentication mechanisms
* High availability infrastructure
* Distributed system architecture
* Audit logs and vote verification
* Blockchain-based vote recording
* Real-time monitoring and analytics

These features will be evaluated as the system evolves.

---

## 9. Learning Goals (Project Context)

Pollaris is also designed as a technical learning project intended to explore modern software engineering practices.

The project aims to include:

* Modern backend development
* Frontend application development
* Version control workflows
* Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)
* Containerized infrastructure
* Scalable system design

The platform will evolve progressively as these practices are implemented.
